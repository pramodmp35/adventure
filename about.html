Our Story

From a small group of friends to a global community of adventurers — here's how we got here.

2012

The Spark

Two friends, one map, and a shared dream of making adventure accessible to everyone. STACKLY was born in a small garage in Boulder, Colorado.

2016

Global Expansion

We launched our first international expeditions to Patagonia and the Himalayas. Our tribe grew from 50 to over 500 explorers in just two years.

2024

Community First

Today, STACKLY is a vibrant community of 5,000+ adventurers. We've curated over 200 unique trips across 30 countries, always prioritizing sustainability and local impact.

Our Vision

To create a world where every person can experience the transformative power of adventure — breaking barriers, building connections, and preserving the wild places we love.

Our Mission

We craft authentic, sustainable, and life-changing travel experiences. We empower local communities, protect ecosystems, and inspire a lifelong passion for exploration.
